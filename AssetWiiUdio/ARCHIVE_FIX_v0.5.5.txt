v0.5.5 archive correction

PK-FFFF index: read from AES-CTR decrypted outer header.
Embedded PK0304 local entries: read from ORIGINAL ciphertext, and reset CTR for each entry.
The v0.5.4 browser accidentally applied CTR twice to member data by using result.Bytes.
The Archive.Source for PK now retains original ciphertext for member export.
DBNK handling remains unchanged.

This is a source-only patch; not compiled or tested with game data here.
